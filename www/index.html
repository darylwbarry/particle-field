Photograph the tray

Spread particles thinly in window L (phone macro) or S (clip-on lens) so few touch. Light the stand from the back or side port. Lay the phone on the deck with the lens over the window, tap the tray to focus, then shoot. Every photo calibrates itself from the dots, so take as many as you need for a good count — 500+ particles gives stable D10/D50/D90.

Stand setup

Used only to correct for particles standing above the tray plane (their outline is closer to the lens). Set it to the setup you used.

    Settings

    With calipers, measure from the left edge of a dot to the left edge of the dot 30 places along (nominal 60.00 mm) and enter that value ÷ 30. This one number sets the absolute scale of every result.

    Changing pitch, threshold, splitting or height correction re-analyses all photos. Size limits, measure and classes update instantly.

    About

    Particle Field

    Measures particle size distributions (about 100–2000 µm) from phone photos of the Particle Field calibration tray. Every photo calibrates itself from the tray's dot grid. Photos are analysed on this device and are not uploaded.
